# 1972 Big Eight generated charts and commentary rosters

This selection covers Colorado, Iowa State, Kansas, Kansas State,
Missouri, Nebraska, Oklahoma and Oklahoma State for 1972. It comprises eight
team metadata files, 24 offense, defense and special-teams chart CSVs, eight
generated cell provenance CSVs and eight commentary roster JSON files.

Bowl Bound independently authored the chart model and encoded its results as
gameplay tables. Rushing and passing inputs come from team and opponent totals
for 27 observed conference games. Kansas and Missouri each have six observed
games. The other teams each have seven. Credit the NCAA and its historical
National Collegiate Sports Services game reports, Colorado Athletics, Iowa
State Athletics, and the SoonerStats 1972 team game log. The model uses authored
play distributions, a fictional Cedar Valley baseline for inherited special-team
and other supplemental results, and authored event, spacing and formation
adjustments. Modern chart reviews informed presentation and model structure.
The charts passed the matched playtest comparison against their predecessor across all eight teams. Historical fit remains incomplete.
Generated cell provenance records chart tokens and virtual coordinates.

The roster JSON files contain factual player names, position groups and a
small number of documented quarterback selections for commentary. The lists
vary in coverage by school. Credit each school's athletics records and
contemporary game evidence, including the Florida State–Kansas information
packet, the original student and regional press coverage, the Sugar Bowl,
HuskerMax, SoonerStats and Sports Reference as a secondary cross-check.
