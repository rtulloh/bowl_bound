generated/generated interactions isolate offense, defense and Priority effects.

Totals alone do not retain DS, shared-roll or select-after-roll dependencies.
Razzle-Dazzle is an artificial engine action, not a chart or observed play family.

Start terminal reduction with direct supported outcomes and at most one selected
conditional lookup. Any further chain, pending decision, unsupported variable,
recovery, penalty or error remains an explicit residual until separately covered.

outcomes retain their full multiplicity. Do not renormalize to known branches,
call primary INT a committed turnover, or call a QT trigger a completed pass.
Check every reduced semantic class against an actual engine terminal subject and
the appropriate independent observer. Include literal boundary/pending-save
controls for supported branches; unsupported observer scope is not a pass.

the four benchmark references. No per-team override or reference-dependent
generation is allowed. Additional teams test the procedure, not the historical
