Milestone 42 / DATA-064, 2026-11-07. Target release: **1.0.0**.
Status: paired prototype, owner review and cohort acceptance pending.
This policy is a declared modeling assumption. It does not certify historical
conditional accuracy.

## Evidence and reuse boundary

Consulted local Paydirt DATA-040 (conditional evidence, shared templates and
fixed INT depths) and DATA-041 (QT blocks and physical weight invariants).
Adapt the separation of conditional evidence from authored allocation, exact
physical-mass accounting and actual Priority checks. Do not reuse NFL play
mapping, weights, rules, private data or a runtime dependency.

A known catch depth on an unidentified pass remains known unconditional
evidence. It supplies no observed conditional depth to Screen, Sprint Out,
Bootleg or Drop Back. Generic “deep pass” wording also does not establish the
designed Drop Back call. The reviewed normalized source currently provides zero
qualifying designed-call/known-catch-depth samples under the explicit marker
contract. This is an identifiability limitation, not evidence of zero depth.

Qualifying observations must be live interceptions for the relevant team and
possession side, with narrative call confidence and the corresponding explicit
designed-call marker. Known interception depth takes precedence over known air
yards; missing locations remain unknown. Observations from another call do not
cross into this conditional population. Razzle-Dazzle is an artificial engine
action and is never a source/chart family.

## Allocation and independent checks

Preserve every existing INT slot and every other football fact: kinds, flags,
formation addresses, Priority exposure, incidence budgets, supplemental and
special teams cells. Fixed catch depths alone change. Variable INT locations
require a separately declared policy and currently fail clearly.

Allocate indivisible physical slot weights to modeled support by minimizing
exact one-dimensional Wasserstein distance, then maximum CDF error, with a
deterministic tie break. A scarce one-slot chart may omit modeled tails; record
that residual rather than forcing a tail or adding an INT. A zero-INT column is
dormant, not an observed zero-depth population.

The source-support/incidence verifier independently selects eligible evidence,
enumerates the 216 offense and 36 defense faces, compares all nondepth facts,
and checks actual Priority-selected depths for all four passing calls and six
formations. A separate exhaustive checker compares every feasible small-slot
assignment against the declared target. Its budget refusal is UNVERIFIED and
cannot satisfy a mandatory gate. Its target document must be bound to the
source/policy receipt; passing an arbitrary target does not establish source fit.

Predeclared component criteria are unchanged slot facts and per-column INT mass;
unchanged Priority kind/operation/source and full 7,776 primary physical mass;
eligible conditional support only; optimal finite encoding under the declared
objective; complete ordinary CSV/provenance round trips; and explicit residual
mass for every unsupported downstream branch. Numerical model differences must
be reported before acceptance, including conditional means/tails, INT_MIN,
field landing, end-zone choices and returns. These criteria do not assert a
historically fitted call distribution.
