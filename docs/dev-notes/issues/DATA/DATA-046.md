## Description

Available developer charts are valuable diagnostic benchmarks, but are not generation targets or permission to redistribute their content. Compare stable candidates under matched conditions and use discrepancies to improve methods that must also work without a benchmark.

## Resolution / verification

- Inventory authorized team/year/edition coverage and normalization compatibility; keep detailed reference material private.
- Freeze source, generator, candidate, runtime, coaching and measurement identities before comparisons.
- Compare exact weighted primary, conditional and terminal outcomes, formation benefit/cost and real-engine behavior under matched conditions.
- Assess both chart sets against the same eligible historical targets and explain discrepancies rather than treating every difference as an error.
- Freeze the resulting method and test transfer to additional teams/seasons without references; distinguish explored benchmarks from held-out validation.
