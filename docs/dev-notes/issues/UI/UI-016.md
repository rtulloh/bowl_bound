## Description

User reports that after the first play is called, the Snap ball button falls below
the bottom of the screen. Growing result/history content should not push the next
primary action out of view. Exact viewport and reproduction remain to be captured
with synthetic teams; this is a user report, not yet an independently reproduced fix.

## Resolution / verification

- Study how Paydirt organizes its scoreboard, field, play selection, primary action,
  results and history. Record the relevant layout patterns and adapt them to Bowl
  Bound rather than copying NFL assumptions or adding a Paydirt runtime dependency.
- Reproduce the first-play overflow with synthetic teams and add a failing browser
  regression before changing the layout. Record viewport dimensions and scroll state.
- Keep score/clock/situation, play selections and Snap ball readily visible together
  at supported desktop sizes before and after plays, without routine page scrolling
  to reach the primary action. Keep result/history/statistics growth from displacing
  core controls; choose compact, bounded or collapsible presentation as appropriate.
- Provide a usable responsive arrangement on smaller screens: the primary action
  remains discoverable and reachable without being buried below accumulated logs.
  Avoid horizontal overflow and sticky controls obscuring content or keyboard focus.
- Preserve accessible keyboard/touch operation, selected states, pending decisions,
  optional clock controls and engine-owned action availability. Do not add routine
  next-play confirmation or duplicate football rules in the frontend.
- Verify initial state, first and repeated plays, long results/history, pending
  penalties/returns/conversions, and save/load with synthetic real-engine browser
  tests at representative desktop and mobile sizes. Assert control visibility and
  viewport placement, not merely DOM presence; retain access to detailed records.
