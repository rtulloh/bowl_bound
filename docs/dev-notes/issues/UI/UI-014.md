## Description

The user requests a coin toss for possession at the beginning of the game, like
Paydirt, to fix the awkward opening kickoff flow for two human players.

The current engine already resolves an offensive-dice opening toss and presents a
kick/receive choice. This ticket requests an explicit, understandable coin-toss
interaction and handoff between players, rather than another hidden or duplicated
possession selection. Use a synthetic matchup when reproducing the flow; no private
play logs or historical chart values are needed.

## Resolution / verification

- Review Paydirt's opening interaction as a usability reference, not a source of
  NFL rules or a runtime dependency. Define the coin call, toss reveal and chooser
  handoff before implementation; document any change from Bowl Bound's currently
  approved offensive-dice toss procedure.
- After selecting teams, guide the humans through the coin toss, identify the
  winner and the player who must choose possession/kickoff, then identify the
  kicking and receiving teams and present the correct kickoff action.
- Keep coin/toss results, legal choices, possession and kickoff scheduling in the
  engine. The API transports the result; the UI must not generate a second result,
  independently assign possession, or execute an extra kickoff.
- Do not prompt for a scrimmage play or show misleading live down/distance while
  setup, toss or kickoff decisions remain outstanding. Make the current phase and
  acting human clear, with consistent kick/receive/kickoff wording.
- Preserve the approved halftime choice, seeded reproducibility, complete observed
  toss evidence and final-state protections. Review the opening flow separately
  from the overtime toss; do not silently change overtime behavior.
- Add synthetic engine/API/UI coverage and real-engine human-vs-human browser
  checks for either toss winner and each supported possession choice. Verify the
  sequence reaches exactly one opening kickoff and then legal scrimmage play.
