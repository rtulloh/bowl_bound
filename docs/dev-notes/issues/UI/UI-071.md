## Description

Players have asked to play games between teams from different years. The engine and API already accept independent season-qualified team IDs and use the designated home team's season for rules (see UI-070), but the setup screen presents two flat, unfiltered dropdowns that do not scale to many seasons and teams and do not make the cross-year choice or governing home season obvious. Define a UI design that keeps same-year play simple, makes independent year/team selection explicit, and leaves room for more seasons, larger catalogs, search, favorites and future matchup modes without reworking the setup flow.

## Resolution / verification

- Document the current setup behavior and its limitations against the real frontend/src/App.jsx and /api/teams catalog shape.
- Specify an information architecture and interaction model for independent home/away year and team selection, including same-year defaults, same-school/different-year matchups and swapped home/away roles.
- Define how the governing home-season rules are surfaced before game start and how unsupported or unreadable selections produce actionable errors.
- Define catalog scaling behavior for many seasons and teams (grouping, filtering, search, keyboard/accessibility, mobile) and a stable season-qualified identity contract.
- Describe a phased implementation path with test-first frontend/API/browser acceptance, save/load identity preservation and explicit non-goals, without implementing behavior in this design ticket.
- Link the design to UI-070 and Milestone 44 and record the Paydirt consultation and what is deliberately not reused.

--- 2026-11-15 — phase 1 implemented ---

Implemented phase 1 of the design: `frontend/src/components/MatchupBuilder.jsx`
provides independent season and team selection per side, a swap control, a
season-scoped team list, a "Use <year> for both teams" shortcut, season-qualified
labels and an inline notice when a team does not exist in a newly chosen year.
Changing one side's season leaves the other side untouched. Defaults remain the
public sample matchup when present, otherwise the newest installed season with
two different teams. The start payload is unchanged.

failing against the old screen) and `frontend/e2e/crossSeason.spec.js` (2 cases).

was added to the browser. Phases 2–4 (searchable picker, rules banner, full
