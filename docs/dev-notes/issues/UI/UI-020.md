## Description

Generic decline buttons do not identify the result being accepted. Historical
play-log entries show only descriptions, without their recorded starting position,
down/distance, yardage or dice. Synthetic examples include an incomplete-pass
penalty alternative and a loss from own 4 that produces a safety.

## Resolution / verification

- Engine-generated decline labels identify prepared facts, without rolling,
  mutating state, predicting recoveries or repeating the prepared play.
- Keep choice IDs, availability and old pending-save digests/continuation stable.
- Show engine-provided before/after positions and yardage in the log; expandable
  audit lists all actual rolls and cells. Do not invent a final state or roll.
- Keep the redundant routine scoreboard prompt out of the visual layout while
  retaining contextual decision/kickoff/final status.
