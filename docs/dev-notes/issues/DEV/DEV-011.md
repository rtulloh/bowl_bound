## Description

Provide two complete, original synthetic teams in the Bowl Bound seasons layout
so future reusable test cases, local play-tests and demonstrations can use them
instead of the imported 2002 Miami and Ohio State teams. The owner wants this
safer default regardless of the word "free" in the historical source filename;
that label alone is not redistribution evidence. Ordinary public tests already
use synthetic fixtures; this request adds reusable, realistically varied sample
teams rather than asserting that current CI requires historical teams.

Paydirt's sample teams may inform the concept and variety, but the new teams must
be formatted for Bowl Bound and have independently authored chart values. Do not
copy historical charts, rename Miami/OSU, or introduce a Paydirt runtime dependency.
Inspect the reference samples and their terms before reusing any actual content.

## Resolution / verification

Implemented and tested in `57b4dd6`. Original CC0 Cedar Valley and Harbor Tech bundles are publicly tracked under an exact seasons/2007 allowlist and selected by default in the normal catalog. Actual YAML/CSV files match the original generator byte-for-byte, cover supported Priority Chart coordinates, and complete deterministic home/away browser games and save continuation. Owner accepted supported 2007 instead of unsupported 2026. Historical charts remain private and are excluded from future release packaging (DEV-012).

See [Milestone 19 acceptance](../../milestone-19.md) for the cumulative gates
and changed expectations. The committed implementation was rechecked with 99
focused Python tests. Awaiting owner play-test confirmation before closure.

- Author two clearly fictional teams with distinct, plausible play styles and
  varied complete offense, defense, supplemental and special-team charts, valid
  recovery ranges, provenance and metadata. Do not attach historical PDF provenance
  or real-player rosters; explicitly identify original synthetic authorship.
- Validate the teams against the current Bowl Bound schema and supported rules,
  including all required roll rows/columns; do not apply NFL/Paydirt chart semantics.
- Make them available as `seasons/<sample-season>/<sample-team>/team.yaml` with
  stable, non-historical identities selectable by the normal backend catalog.
  Preserve a redistributable tracked source or narrowly allowlisted sample paths
  so a fresh checkout can reproduce this setup. Never unignore seasons broadly
  or overwrite/delete the owner's existing historical teams.
- Record explicit redistribution terms for original sample content and document
  installation/generation, selection, and the fact that the teams are fictional.
  Sample content must not derive from licensed historical charts even indirectly.
- Use these samples as the default for reusable local/browser/full-game examples
  and future test scenarios that need selectable teams. Keep small synthetic unit
  fixtures where clearer. Normal checks must run with Miami/OSU/private PDFs absent.
- Add synthetic tests first for loading/catalog selection and validation, then
  deterministic games covering rules, clock, score, statistics, traces and save/
  resume. Verify both sides are playable and distinct without chart interpretation
  in the frontend. Preserve fixtures capable of exercising rare outcomes.
- Audit committed sample sources/generated outputs for provenance and historical
  content, and update player/developer setup guidance and attribution in the same
  change. **Blocking publication prerequisite:** create, validate and document both
  teams before adding/linking a GitHub remote or publishing any repository.
  Milestone 24 acceptance must verify this ticket is resolved; it is not optional
  post-publication work. Do not publish historical teams on the basis of a filename
  or prior assumption; their distribution remains a separate reviewed decision.
