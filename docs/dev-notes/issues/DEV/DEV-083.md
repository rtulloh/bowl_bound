serial coverage step also exhausts the later 240-minute bound. Keep the shared
source limit at 240 minutes and native jobs at 45 minutes. Preserve every test,
combined branch coverage and its threshold, other gate commands, failure
propagation and publication separation. Update exact workflow contract assertions;
demonstrate their two expected failures before implementation, then run focused
and applicable full regression checks before pushing. Record measured hosted
completion and actual artifact checks before claiming upstream success. The
comments below preserve the earlier 120- and 240-minute serial proposals.
