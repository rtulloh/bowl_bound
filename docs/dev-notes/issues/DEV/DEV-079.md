The 1.0.0 generation/validation work reached the disk-capacity reserve and actual
write failures. The owner requested cleanup of unused Bowl Bound sibling folders
and is coordinating separate Paydirt/personal-folder cleanup. Scope this operation
to verified local caches and idle Bowl Bound checkouts; preserve all active work.

## Resolution / verification

- Reclaim rebuildable download cache without changing installed dependencies,
  source, charts, saves, audit evidence or active servers. Retain the generation
  reserve; do not weaken its guard to force execution.
- Audit every proposed checkout's Git status, HEAD, preserving named branch and
  open files before retirement. Preserve unmerged commits through their refs.
- Archive all non-rebuildable ignored/untracked data, including private evidence
  and test results, before removing a checkout. Verify every archived file's
  SHA-256, archive identity and symlink targets; never dereference shared
  dependency symlinks into a destructive operation.
- Retire only reviewed idle linked worktrees. Retain active research/integration
  trees, live previews and the independent public repository with unmerged work.
  Do not delete branches or tags, or touch Paydirt's separate cleanup.
- Verify final folders/worktree metadata, retained refs, archives, active servers
  and available disk space. Keep recovery manifests private and regenerate the
  public-safe issue index. This operation changes no gameplay behavior.
