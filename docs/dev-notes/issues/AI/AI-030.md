## Description

When the deferred coffin-corner punt rule is implemented, fourth-down coaching must estimate that punt's field-position outcome alongside ordinary punts, field goals, and short-yardage go-for-it attempts. A favorable coffin-corner option should raise the field-goal make-rate threshold needed to prefer a field-goal attempt.

## Resolution / verification

- After coffin-corner punts are engine-supported, synthetic fourth-down comparisons include ordinary punt, coffin-corner punt, field goal, and legal scrimmage calls.
- A synthetic boundary case proves a strong coffin-corner outcome can prefer punt over a low-probability field goal while a higher-probability field goal still wins.
- Normal games keep the option unavailable until the underlying coffin-corner punt rule is explicitly enabled.
