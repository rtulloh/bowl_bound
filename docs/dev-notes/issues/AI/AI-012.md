## Description

The shared rule is field goal if 30 yards or closer, punt if more than 2 to go, otherwise go, with only a trailing-late exception; conversions always kick. No tier uses aggression or score for these choices, and no last-seconds field goal or last-snap long pass exists for Easy or Medium. Paydirt gates these with aggression thresholds, field-position tiers, score and time, and selects two-point tries by margin late. Known Paydirt defects AI-013 (short yardage ignores a big lead) should be avoided from the start.

## Resolution / verification

- Named rules: trailing late and needing a touchdown never punts or kicks; large lead late does not gamble; kick ranges come from chart or rules-profile facts, not NFL numbers; two-point selection by margin in the final period when allowed. Matrix tests per tier and side; audits show the distribution. New policy versions for changed behavior.
