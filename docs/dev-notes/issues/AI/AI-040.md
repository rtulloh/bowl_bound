## Description

A review reports that Razzle-Dazzle hypothetical continuations lose modifiers
already committed to the pending play. The sampled sandbox in
bowl_bound/coaching_evaluation.py (review location near line 517) omits the
bounds declaration. The committed hurry flag is also lost when analysis reads
the next-snap view instead of the pending session's current-play context.
Capture the authoritative committed modifiers in razzle_context and replay
them in both sampled and exact evaluators, subject to verification against the
current implementation.

The review's synthetic bounds example declares bounds:oob before RZDZ. A
natural out-of-bounds zero-yard result and an in-bounds two-yard result have
different actual engine costs: the latter becomes minus three yards after the
five-yard declaration cost. Defensive v11 analysis can select the zero-yard
result because its sandbox does not apply that declaration, reversing the
correct defensive preference. A mirrored ordering distinguishes the reported
v10/v11 behavior without sampling variance.

For hurried RZDZ, RegulationGame.snap clears view.hurried, while the pending
PlaySession retains its committed hurry flag. Hypothetical S/R penalty
resolution consequently uses normal tables although the actual pending engine
play uses hurried tables. This is an AI/human outcome-parity issue, not a request
to alter chart probabilities or football rules.

RZDZ is an artificial choice action, not a play column or cell in team charts.
