## Description

A late-half computer offense can attempt a field goal from deep in its own territory on an early down. The conventional distance function already returns yards-to-goal plus 17, but ChartAwareCoach adds field_goal to every late-half candidate mixture without the public maximum-range guard. Even a poor estimate retains exploration weight. Reproduce with fictional teams on third-and-long at own 26, Q2 with 70 seconds remaining; the attempt distance is 91 yards.

## Resolution / verification

- Write failing synthetic regressions for both possession directions and all normal difficulties, including the public 55/56-yard boundary, failed-analysis fallback and fourth-down controls.
- Apply the existing shared public field-goal range to AI candidate eligibility before sampling; preserve engine legality, chart range semantics and in-range late-half opportunities.
- Version future coaching, preserve explicit older policy streams and strict saved-game replay, and validate API/audit registrations and complete-game decisions.
