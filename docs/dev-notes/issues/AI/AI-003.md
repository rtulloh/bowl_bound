## Description

coaching_evaluation._potential adds a flat +2 for possession and negates it for the opponent, so every change of possession costs at least 4 preference units regardless of spot while 40 punt yards are worth 1.6. On synthetic sample charts Hard chose punt about 2% on 4th-and-10 from its own 20 and scored a made field goal from the 25 as negative utility. The 32-game public audit shows Hard punting twice versus 26-33 for other policies. This is a valuation defect, not acceptable aggression. hard-v1 is still uncommitted, so it can be corrected in place; once committed any change needs a new policy version.

## Resolution / verification

- Synthetic test: Hard on 4th-and-10 from its own 20 in the first quarter, tied, selects punt in a clear majority of play_number variants. Test: a certain field goal from the 25 has positive mean utility exceeding every scrimmage candidate. Test: a certain touchdown from the 1 outranks a certain zero-yard gain by a clear margin. Potential depends on field position with possession value near zero deep in own territory. Document the units in docs/coaching.md and update docs/dev-notes/coaching-audit-m21.md after rerunning the 32-game comparison.
