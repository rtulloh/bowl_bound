## Description

A public-safe synthetic Medium v9 observation for a tied first quarter, fourth-and-13 at the opponent 33, with punt and field_goal both offered, can select a scrimmage call. After an accepted offensive five-yard penalty replaying fourth down, the same policy can select another scrimmage call at fourth-and-18 from the opponent 38. Owner play-testing identifies punt as the required conservative choice for this early long-yardage situation. This is a coaching-policy defect, not a chart or dice rule.

## Resolution / verification

- Write a synthetic regression for Medium v9 at tied early-Q1 fourth-and-13 from the opponent 33 with punt/field goal offered, proving it selects punt rather than a scrimmage call.
- Cover the replayed fourth-and-18 situation after an accepted offensive penalty, without relying on private teams or play logs.
- Preserve versioned save/coaching determinism and document the intended early fourth-down policy.
