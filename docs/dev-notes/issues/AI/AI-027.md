## Description

The prior coaching guard used a generic 30-yards-to-goal cutoff. It can attempt
unreasonable long field goals and can punt from viable range, such as opponent
31. A chart-derived replacement would give the CPU kick knowledge unavailable to
the human player. The owner instead chose a shared public policy based on attempt
distance, yards to gain, score/clock, and declared difficulty aggression. The
engine continues to resolve every selected kick from its chart.

## Resolution / verification

- Synthetic regressions cover every public distance-band boundary, short-yardage
  exceptions, late trailing behavior, and identical decisions for strong/weak
  synthetic kick charts at the same public situation.
- Fourth-down selection never evaluates a kick chart to choose field goal, punt,
  or go-for-it.
- Punt, field goal, and scrimmage decisions remain legal and deterministic for a
  saved public situation. Actual kick outcomes remain engine/chart-owned.
