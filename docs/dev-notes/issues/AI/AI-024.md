## Description

Review of 5294eb8..44d3438 reproduced a strategy regression in `bowl_bound/difficulty.py:210-221`. For any trailing offense with 1–20 seconds left in Q2/Q4, the policy returns hail_mary before inspecting score margin, field position or the field-goal chart. This overrides the score/chart-aware selection added for AI-012.

Synthetic reproduction: make a Home chart whose field-goal entries all reach 70 yards; observe Q4, 0:10, second down at the opponent 5, trailing by one, with field_goal and hail_mary offered. Easy, Medium and Hard v9 all unconditionally choose Hail Mary, despite the offered guaranteed three-point kick winning the game. The branch also runs at halftime and when the offense has additional useful choices.

`test_v3_last_snap_prefers_the_engine_offered_hail_mary` fixes the answer to Hail Mary while trailing by one without checking kick viability. `test_field_goal_estimate_uses_bowl_bound_chart_range_at_longer_distance` tests estimates directly, bypassing the actual offense selector that returns early. Together these tests do not verify the ticket's last-seconds field-goal behavior.

Assigned to milestone 23 as follow-up to committed milestone 22.

## Resolution / verification

- Make the last-play choice depend on whether a field goal suffices and its engine-evaluated chart viability; retain Hail Mary when a touchdown is required or a kick cannot help.
- Test the real offense selector on both sides and all tiers for deficits of 1, 2, 3 and 4+, kick range/success differences, and Q2 versus Q4.
- Update contrary test expectations with the score/chart-aware rationale and version any changed coaching policy.
