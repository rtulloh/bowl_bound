## Description

A committed QB Sneak is narrated with the running-back actor because the commentary role resolver treats every ordinary rushing credit as a running-back play. The called QB Sneak is preserved in the engine report; the commentary actor should use that identity without changing the resolved Line Plunge trace, yardage or statistics.

## Resolution / verification

- Use a synthetic roster with distinct quarterback and running-back names; QB Sneak narration selects the quarterback, including zero gain, negative gain and touchdown outcomes.
- Keep ordinary running-play actors, QR and QT handling, stored descriptions, dice, statistics and replay digests unchanged.
