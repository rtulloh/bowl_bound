## Description

A punt or kickoff with a chart penalty result and reroll prints its trace while
the procedure is pending, then repeats the same trace after penalty acceptance.
It can look like multiple kicks even though the engine executed one kick
procedure with a reroll and, when accepted, a re-kick from the enforced spot.

## Resolution / verification

- Synthetic terminal regressions distinguish one punt/kickoff procedure, its
  documented reroll, its accepted-penalty re-kick where applicable, and its
  final report without duplicate-looking play logs.
- The terminal explicitly labels a discarded original kick, the penalty reroll,
  and any accepted-penalty re-kick so players know which chart result becomes the
  live kick result.
- Structured trace evidence and save replay remain complete and unchanged.
