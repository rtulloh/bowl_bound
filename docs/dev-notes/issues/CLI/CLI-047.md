## Description

Owner alpha play-test feedback prefers role-specific Paydirt-style help: offense shows up to three ranked legal plays with concise numeric chart estimates and a brief situational tip/top recommendation; defense shows one direct Recommended: key - name call instead of repeated general advice paragraphs. Use Bowl Bound engine-owned recommendations and legal labels. Offensive percentages are conditional numeric-chart target rates, not full-engine success or conversion odds against a defense. This is discussion during the local release pause, not authorization to resume release work or change gameplay/default selection.

## Resolution / verification

- At an eligible Easy human offensive play-selection prompt, show up to three ranked callable plays, with key/name, target yards, conditional target-reaching percentage, positive-gain percentage, average yards and evaluated chart coverage. Preserve the existing numeric-only estimation/exclusion semantics and honest limited/unavailable data handling.
- Keep a concise visible chart-only/exclusions explanation so partial chart coverage is not presented as full-game success probability; retain a brief relevant situational tip and identify the leading recommendation. Avoid redundant explanations that merely repeat the same figures.
- At an eligible Easy human defensive selection prompt, show one concise Recommended: key - name defense using only currently offered Bowl Bound legal actions and labels. Do not print general situation/clock paragraphs at every ordinary defensive prompt.
- Produce a concrete defensive recommendation from public situation and legal formations without inspecting a concealed CPU call, claiming learned tendencies or importing NFL/Paydirt formation mappings.
- Preserve Easy-on-by-default and Z toggle behavior, Medium/Hard restrictions and pending-choice/finished-game boundaries; show no duplicated recommendation block after toggling.
- Keep recommendations advisory: do not silently change Enter/default action behavior, consume gameplay/coaching RNG, or alter game state, save compatibility or dice distributions.
- Consult Paydirt, review synthetic tests before red runs and verify recommendation legality, hidden-call independence, repeated display/save-load stability and owner CLI acceptance before resolving.
