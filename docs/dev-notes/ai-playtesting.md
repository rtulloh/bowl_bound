# Human play-test checklist for coaching

Use this checklist to review a computer decision during a real game. It adds
qualitative evidence to automated audits; a score or a few games do not measure
overall coaching quality.

## Before playing

- Record the Bowl Bound commit, team IDs, difficulty/policy, human side and seed
  in a local note.
- Keep historical charts, saves, rosters and detailed play logs private. Public
  tickets and examples must use synthetic or approved sample teams.

## Review decisions before the dice

For a call that seems questionable, record the situation, legal choices shown by
the engine, the computer's choice and the alternative you expected. Consider:

- Does the call use a relevant strength of the team's chart?
- Is it plausible for the down, distance, field position, score and time?
- Does the computer vary calls in comparable situations, or repeat one without a
  clear reason?
- Does it choose only from actions the engine offers?

Then record the result separately. A sound decision can fail on the chart; a poor
decision can succeed. Judge the choice using what was known before the dice, not
the play's outcome or final score.

## Record and follow up

Keep the observation short: commit and game setup, situation, legal menu, selected
call, expected alternative, and the result in a separate field. Keep raw private
evidence local. If the same decision concern recurs, create or update a ticket
with a synthetic reproduction. Close a resolved coaching ticket only after the
owner confirms the behavior in play-testing, following the [ticket conventions](issues/README.md).
