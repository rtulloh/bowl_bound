This extends [Milestone 42](milestone-42.md#whole-cohort-passing-cell-audit)
and DATA-064. It audits the actual ordinary chart files after completed parent
calibration and the final conditional-depth stage. It does not replace that

## Numerical contract before cohort computation

Per team, inventory 240 raw Screen/Sprint Out/Boot-leg/Drop Back cells, 60 Option
cells and 90 B/QR/QT supplemental cells. Check all 630 scrimmage/supplemental cells
for token/flag/color unions, contiguous runs and identical columns across calls
and formations. Every coordinate must occur exactly once. Each offensive or
supplemental column has physical mass 216; each defensive column has mass 36.
Raw conditional-column mass is not unconditional event incidence.

INT locations must be fixed and belong to the independently qualified call/side
support. The separate exhaustive gate checks the complete finite allocation,
including canonical ties. Unknown designed calls never become observed support.
For Option, retain called play five and resolved seven/eight, the offensive
reroll denominator and cancellation of original ordinary defense. No Razzle-Dazzle
source/chart family is introduced.

All semantic tokens must round-trip through the ordinary codec without changing
kind, fixed/variable payload, priority, sideline or penalty facts. Unmapped kinds,
missing cells/support mappings and invalid payloads refuse the census. Incomplete
passes remain distinct from numeric zero/negative completions. A QT negative
numeric result is a sack; zero/positive QT and QR numeric results are rushes.
B retains its originating classification. These annotations alone do not qualify
a complete dependent event.

For fixed gain diagnostics, freeze explicitly named source envelopes from the
same conditioned provider population: unidentified completed passes for direct
passing columns, rush/completion union for Option and B, pure rush for QR and
nonnegative QT, sacks for negative QT. Exclude no-plays and tries; retain missing
yardage counts. An empty population stays unknown. Flag every number outside
that source range for review; retain every number inside as unidentified-call
evidence, not a conditional-fit pass. These are source diagnostics, not universal
yardage caps or legality rules: a defensive additive contribution is not a whole
play gain, and a long Screen completion can include a run after catch.

Measure exact primary Priority over all five called plays and six formations
against an independent transcription. Retain variable/conditional/pending bins
over denominator 7,776; do not renormalize them away or draw a fictional die.
Option, B/QR/QT, INT, penalties, recovery and field/end-zone consequences need
their separately frozen actual-engine observation panels before terminal claims.
